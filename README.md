# 📎 Project Name: Shareek

---

## 📌 Project Overview:
**Shareek** is a modern web application built with React and Vite, designed to solve the chronic housing and accommodation challenges faced by university students and expatriates across Egypt. Moving beyond traditional listing directories, Shareek features an intelligent **Roommate Matching System** that connects students based on their lifestyle habits, study preferences, and daily routines—ensuring a compatible, stress-free shared living experience alongside affordable housing options.

## 🎯 Project Objectives:
* **Centralized Student Housing:** Provide verified housing listings categorized by proximity to Egyptian universities and major public transit (Metro stations).
* **Smart Roommate Matching:** Minimize roommate incompatibility by matching students based on study habits, sleeping schedules, cleanliness, and smoking preferences.
* **Affordable Living:** Facilitate cost-sharing by connecting students looking to co-rent apartments or fill vacant beds.
* **Fast & Responsive Interface:** Deliver a clean, mobile-responsive user interface using Vite, React, and Bootstrap.

---

## 📦 Project Scope:
* **Student Onboarding & Lifestyle Questionnaire:** Multi-step form capturing sleeping routines, noise tolerance during study hours, budget, and university details.
* **Roommate Discovery & Compatibility Scoring:** Dynamic profile cards presenting calculated compatibility percentages and key lifestyle badges.
* **Property Listings & Detailed Views:** Housing cards showcasing photo carousels, pricing (per bed/room/entire flat), and student-essential amenities (Wi-Fi, AC, study desks).
* **Interactive Map Integration:** Visual search using Leaflet/OpenStreetMap to explore housing options near target universities and transit hubs.
* **Connection & Mock Booking Flow:** Direct roommate connection requests and an interactive booking/reservation flow with date selection.
* **User Portal:** Dashboard for tracking saved listings, outgoing roommate requests, and personal profile management.
